What it does

Everything you need on stage.
Nothing you don't.

⚡

Per-channel routing

Send each stem to its own physical output on interfaces with up to 32 channels. FOH gets a real mix, not a stereo bounce.

📡

Browser-based remote

Phones, tablets, laptops — anyone on the same Wi‑Fi opens a URL, scans a QR, and gets a live transport. No app install, no extra software.

🗣️

Guide Builder

Build a spoken guide track for any song. Drop cues like "Verse", "Chorus" or "Intro 2 3 4" right on the waveform and StemDeck bakes them into a guide stem timed to the song's tempo. Send it to its own output so the whole band hears what's coming in their in-ears.

🥁

Click tracks & lead-ins

Two-point BPM calculator from any two downbeats. Generates a click track with configurable count-in measures. Routed to its own channel for in-ear feeds.

🔁

Vamp / loop sections

Mark a region. Loop it indefinitely. Release with one tap and the song picks up where the band left off — no manual re-cue.

🎚️

Pitch & tempo per song

Transpose ±12 semitones. Tempo from 0.5× to 2×. Click and count-in stems automatically bypass pitch but follow tempo.

📜

Auto-cued setlists

When one song ends, the next loads automatically. Hit GO when you're ready. The band sees what's coming, the operator doesn't have to scramble.

💾

Mix export

Render a stereo mix for band rehearsal — sample-identical to live playback. What they hear in their cans is what they hear at the gig.

🎹

MIDI control

Drive transport from any MIDI controller — foot switch, pad, keyboard. CC mappings are configurable per show.
